## 摘要
自由能计算通常用于研究无偏分子动力学无法触及的分子过程，但其效用最终取决于能否知道其预测何时以及多大程度上可信。因此，不确定性估计对于区分自由能曲线的真实物理特征与因有限模拟数据或采样不足而产生的人为假象至关重要。高斯过程已成为重建自由能曲线及预测不确定性的强大框架。然而，现有实现通常以固定超参数和观测噪声为条件，使得预测不确定性无法适应模拟数据的信息内容。在此，我们开发了一个广义分层高斯过程框架，以考虑这些被忽略的不确定性来源。应用于肽-脂质膜相互作用的伞形采样和扩展拉格朗日元动力学表明，所得不确定性估计在广泛的采样和数据条件下跟踪重建误差。

## Abstract
Free energy calculations are routinely used to study molecular processes inaccessible to unbiased molecular dynamics, but their utility ultimately depends on knowing when and how much their predictions can be trusted. Uncertainty estimation is therefore essential for distinguishing genuine physical features of a free energy profile from artifacts arising from limited simulation data or inadequate sampling. Gaussian processes have emerged as a powerful framework for reconstructing free energy profiles together with predictive uncertainties. However, existing implementations typically condition on fixed hyperparameters and observation noise, preventing predictive uncertainties from adapting to the information content of the simulation data. Here, we develop a generalized hierarchical Gaussian process framework that accounts for these neglected sources of uncertainty. Applications to umbrella sampling and extended Lagrangian metadynamics of peptide-lipid membrane interactions demonstrate that the resulting uncertainty estimates track reconstruction errors across a wide range of sampling and data conditions.
