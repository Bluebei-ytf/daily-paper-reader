## 摘要
周环反应为量子模拟提供了严格的测试，因为其机理由轨道对称性控制，并涉及关联过渡态。在本工作中，我们采用变分量子本征求解器（VQE）结合先前建立的基于对称匹配片段的对称引导活动空间选择协议（SMF-VQE），模拟了涉及扩展π共轭和多键的复杂体系中的Diels-Alder和Alder-ene反应。尽管当前协议获得的绝对电子能量与使用CCSD方法计算的值存在显著偏差，但对称引导活动空间在能量差中实现了偏差的大量抵消。因此，反应能的预测误差（相对于CCSD）小于1 kcal/mol，而活化能的再现误差约为5至6 kcal/mol。该对称引导协议还将活动空间选择的大组合空间缩减为每个反应的一个对称一致选择。

## Abstract
Pericyclic reactions provide stringent tests for quantum simulations because their mechanisms are governed by orbital symmetry and involve correlated transition states. In this work, we employ the variational quantum eigensolver (VQE) combined with a previously established symmetry-guided active-space selection protocol based on symmetry-matched fractions (SMF-VQE) to simulate Diel-Alder and Alder-ene reactions in complex systems involving extended pi-conjugation and multiple bonding. Although absolute electronic energies obtained from the current protocol exhibit significant deviations from the values computed using CCSD method, the symmetry-guided active spaces yield substantial cancellation of deviations in the energy differences. As a result, reaction energies are predicted with error (relative to CCSD) less than one kcal per mol, while activation energies are reproduced within about five to six kcal per mol. The symmetry-guided protocol also reduces the large combinatorial space of active-space choices to a single symmetry-consistent selection for each reaction.
